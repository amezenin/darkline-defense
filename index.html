Privacy Policy for Darkline Defense

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Darkline Defense is developed and published by Mezhenon Production. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use Darkline Defense.

Darkline Defense is intended for users aged 18 and older.

1. Information We Collect

Darkline Defense does not require you to create an account and does not directly request personal information such as your name, postal address, phone number, or payment card information.

However, third-party services integrated into the game may automatically collect or process certain technical information necessary to provide advertising, purchases, security, fraud prevention, consent management, and service functionality.

This information may include:

  • Device identifiers
  • Advertising identifiers where permitted
  • IP address
  • Device type and operating system
  • App version
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Advertising interactions
  • Diagnostic and crash-related information
  • Purchase and transaction status
  • Consent and privacy preference information

2. Advertising

Darkline Defense may display optional rewarded advertisements using Google AdMob.

Rewarded advertisements are voluntary. Players may choose to watch an advertisement in exchange for an in-game benefit.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, advertising interactions, and other technical data in order to provide, measure, secure, and personalize advertisements where legally permitted.

Depending on your location and privacy choices, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

More information about how Google processes data can be found at:

Google Privacy Policy

Google Advertising Policies and Technologies

3. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by law, Darkline Defense may use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent or present privacy choices before advertising services are used.

Users in applicable regions may be able to choose whether certain forms of advertising data processing are permitted.

Where supported, privacy choices may also be reviewed or changed from within the game's Settings menu.

4. Premium Purchase

Darkline Defense may offer a one-time Premium purchase through Google Play.

Premium may remove the need to watch rewarded advertisements and may grant equivalent in-game benefits automatically.

Payments are processed entirely by Google Play Billing.

Mezhenon Production does not receive, process, or store your full credit card number, bank account information, or other payment credentials.

Google Play may provide the game with information required to determine whether a Premium purchase is owned, active, refunded, cancelled, or otherwise valid.

Information about Google Play payments and privacy can be found in the Google Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Local Game Data

Darkline Defense stores game progress and settings locally on your device.

This may include information such as:

  • Game progress
  • Unlocked content
  • Upgrade levels
  • In-game currency
  • Game settings
  • Language settings
  • Premium entitlement cache
  • Advertising reward state

This information is primarily stored locally and is used to operate the game.

6. No User Accounts

Darkline Defense does not require registration and does not provide its own user account system.

Google Play services may use your Google account independently for purchases, licensing, installation, and other Google Play functionality.

7. Analytics

Mezhenon Production does not currently operate its own remote analytics service for Darkline Defense.

Third-party services such as Google AdMob and Google Play may process technical, diagnostic, advertising, or transaction-related information as described in their respective privacy policies.

8. Data Sharing

Mezhenon Production does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers when required to provide functionality such as:

  • Advertising
  • Consent management
  • Google Play purchases
  • Fraud prevention
  • Security
  • App distribution

These services operate under their own privacy policies and legal obligations.

9. Data Retention

Locally stored game data remains on your device until it is deleted by the game, cleared through device settings, or removed when the application is uninstalled, subject to Android and Google Play behavior.

Information processed by Google or other third-party services may be retained according to their own retention policies.

10. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to minimize unnecessary collection and exposure of information.

However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Children's Privacy

Darkline Defense is intended exclusively for users aged 18 and older.

The game is not directed toward children and is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18.

Mezhenon Production does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

If we become aware that personal information from a child has been collected in violation of applicable law, appropriate steps will be taken to address it.

12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your country or region, you may have rights concerning personal information, including rights to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or withdrawal of consent.

Much of the data associated with advertising and Google Play services is controlled or processed by Google. Requests relating specifically to Google's services may need to be submitted directly to Google.

For matters controlled directly by Mezhenon Production, you may contact us using the email address below.

13. European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Similar Regions

Where applicable, advertising consent and privacy choices may be presented before advertising-related processing occurs.

Depending on the user's choices and applicable law, advertising may be personalized, non-personalized, limited, or unavailable.

Users may be provided with a way to review privacy options from within Darkline Defense where required.

14. Third-Party Services

Darkline Defense may use services provided by Google, including:

  • Google Play
  • Google Play Billing
  • Google AdMob
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

These services may process information according to Google's own terms and privacy policies.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Darkline Defense changes, when new services are added, or when legal or platform requirements change.

The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be changed when this Privacy Policy is materially updated.
